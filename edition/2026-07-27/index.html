    LoopFollow 7.0 is Here!¶

    This is a major update with a fresh new look, easier setup, smarter alarms, and lots of improvements.

    Requires iOS 18 or later. If your device can't run iOS 18, your current version will continue to work.

    Highlights¶

    • Easier setup with a new onboarding guide for Nightscout and Dexcom Share
    • Beautiful new interactive charts that let you pan, zoom, and inspect your data
    • Custom alarm sounds so you can choose alerts that work best for you
    • Smarter alarms with improved predictive low alerts and fewer unnecessary notifications
    • Better visibility with improved warnings for common connection and configuration issues
    • Search settings and menus to quickly find what you're looking for
    • And more!

    Plus… This release also includes numerous bug fixes, improved reliability, more accurate reporting, and other behind-the-scenes enhancements.

    👉 See the full release notes on GitHub and the post on Facebook.

    Put Tidepool in Your Diabetes Tool Kit¶

    We encourage use of Tidepool as part of your diabetes management suite of apps. Never mind that it’s gorgeous- it also has many useful features, such as the ability to hover over a bolus shown on the plot and see whether is was manually requested or automatically delivered from the algorithm. This works for Loop and for Trio and is very helpful when trying to determine if an accidental bolus occurred.

    Five AI Models, Three Users, One Finding: Part Two in a Series on AI and Diabetes Management¶

    Tim Street continued his analysis of AI’s ability to give cogent diabetes advice, this time focused on settings. Three AID system users x five AI models; 1,950 chances for AI to “do the right thing”. The findings were disappointing -a bit shocking, in fact. The article explains why and also gives tips for those who try using AI for settings analysis and advice themselves, as well as for those who are building AI tools for such.

    Our best advice:

    • Learn how your AID system works and what its settings do
    • Ask for help from the online community whenever you need it or just want a second opinion
    • Read discussions on Facebook, Discord, and Zulip (links!)
    • Watch our helpful YouTube videos (link!)
    • Join our bimonthly Open Mic sessions (or our Italian Open Mic, if you speak Italian!) (links!)

    We do not recommend using AI to choose your settings or calculate meal macros. If you do use AI for diabetes-related advice, don't assume its recommendations are correct. Compare them with what you already know about your diabetes, and monitor your glucose closely to make sure the results match your expectations. Ultimately, your own experience, your data, and your understanding of your system are your best guides.

    Insulin-Dependent Earthquake Victims Need Our Support¶

    The devastating earthquakes that struck last month in Venezuela have left countless families struggling not only with the loss and destruction caused by the disaster, but also with the urgent need for life-sustaining medical supplies. For many people living with Type 1 diabetes, access to insulin and essential diabetes supplies was already difficult due to economic hardships. In the aftermath of these earthquakes, obtaining these necessities has become nearly impossible.

    Guerreros Azules, an organization that helps families with type 1 diabetes, has launched a GoFundMe campaign to help provide insulin, medications, and critical diabetes supplies to affected families.

    Every donation, no matter the size, can make a life-saving difference.

    Help DPAC Protect Diabetes Research¶

    Government-funded research in diabetes has been critical to understanding the disease and developing innovative therapies and technologies. Grants and research funding applications should be reviewed as part of an independent, peer review process where the only goal is advancing science and improving health.

    Read the petition text and sign it here. For US only.
